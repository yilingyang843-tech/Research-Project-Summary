**Report:** This report introduces the temperature prediction framework of Wenzhou Kean University. It combines random forest regression with seasonal decomposition and Holt-Winters exponential smoothing to explain data preprocessing, the mathematical principles of the model, and the method of combining prediction results through translation and scale adjustment. It also analyzes and evaluates the results and the limitations of the model.

**Python Notebook:** This notebook processes historical temperature records and ERA5 meteorological data, constructs periodic and lag features, trains a random forest model for hourly temperature prediction; implements temperature estimation based on Holt-Winters, integrates the two types of predictions through translation and scale adjustment, and calculates evaluation indicators, visualizes temperature changes and prediction results.
