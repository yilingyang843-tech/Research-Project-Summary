**Report:** Introduces the SA-AdaAdv-FedGNN federated graph neural network framework for diabetes classification, explains the mathematical principles of patient similarity graph construction, adaptive graph learning, adversarial representation alignment, and attention aggregation. Through benchmark comparisons and component ablation analyses, the roles of each module are discussed, and issues such as data distribution differences, class imbalance, and model limitations are addressed.

**Python:** Completes the preprocessing of diabetes health indicator data, simulates 5 clients with different data distributions and builds KNN patient similarity graphs; implements the training of local graph neural networks, adaptive graph learning, adversarial training, and the process of dynamically calculating aggregation weights based on client statistics information. Compares 5 federated learning models, calculates accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and AUC, and visualizes the training process and client aggregation weights.
